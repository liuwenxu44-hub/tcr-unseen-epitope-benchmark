# Release contents

This is a curated scientific release rather than a mirror of the internal research workspace.

Included:

- confirmatory data-construction, modeling, scoring, and statistical code;
- frozen configurations and manifests;
- compact derived benchmark cohorts needed by the published workflow;
- registered model and training-reference artifacts;
- frozen internal and external result summaries;
- end-user setup, reproducibility, model-card, limitations, and prediction documentation.

Excluded:

- raw VDJdb and IEDB exports;
- manuscript drafting and submission-production files;
- internal project-management and server-maintenance utilities;
- superseded or exploratory result packages;
- caches, local paths, editor files, and manually edited figure source files.

The complete internal archive remains separate from this public release.
