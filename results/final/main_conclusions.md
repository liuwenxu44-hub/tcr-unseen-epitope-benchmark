# Frozen Main Conclusions

1. After exact degree matching, the TCR-only Logistic control is at random level: all four OOF metrics are 0.500000 and all Holm-adjusted permutation p-values are 1.0.
2. Orthogonalized-interaction Logistic exceeds Additive-only Logistic in all 25/25 train-resample by test-resample combinations for all four metrics.
3. The primary overall Macro AUPRC difference is +0.016785, with 95% CI [0.006463, 0.027123] and Holm-adjusted p=0.009999.
4. The 95% confidence intervals for pooled AUROC, pooled AUPRC, and Macro AUROC cross zero; these three effects must not be described as statistically significant.
5. The absolute signal is weak and does not support claims of high predictive performance or state-of-the-art performance.
6. Earlier random/unseen analyses, leakage-affected runs, and explicitly superseded artifacts are retained only as exploratory records and are excluded from the core conclusions.
