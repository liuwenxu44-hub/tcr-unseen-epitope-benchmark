# Third-party data and artifact terms

The root MIT license applies to original software code and code documentation
authored for this repository. It does not relicense third-party source data.

## Source databases

- VDJdb records remain subject to the terms and citation requirements of
  VDJdb and its source publications.
- IEDB records remain subject to the terms and citation requirements of IEDB
  and its source publications.
- Raw VDJdb and IEDB exports are not distributed through this Git repository.
  Reproducible download scripts, source URLs, checksums, filtering rules, and
  manifests are provided instead.

Derived datasets and result artifacts are provided for research
reproducibility only where redistribution is permitted. Their inclusion does
not grant rights to the underlying third-party records. Users are responsible
for reviewing the current source-database terms and citing the database
articles and original publications.

## Models and research use

The registered model checkpoints and prediction utilities are research
artifacts. Their scores are research ranking scores, not calibrated clinical
probabilities. They must not be used for clinical decisions, patient
selection, treatment selection, or as a substitute for experimental
validation.
